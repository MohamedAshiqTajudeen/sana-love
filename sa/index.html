A letter carried through the quiet

For Sana Marzooqa

from Mohamed Ashiq

“I made something for you.”

A

Sana,

You once asked me if my love for you
would fade someday.

Honestly, I don’t know what tomorrow will bring.
But I know one thing about my heart—

it keeps choosing you.

I don’t want you only for a moment,
or only when everything is perfect.

I want you through the messy days,
the quiet days,
the days when we don’t understand each other.

So whenever that little fear comes back,
come back to this:

You are loved.
You are chosen.
And you are not someone I want to lose.

I’m yours, Sana.
Today, tomorrow, and every day I get to love you.

— Ashiq

So whenever that little fear comes back…

remember this.

You are loved.

You are chosen.

And my heart still chooses you.

— always.

I’m yours, Sana.

Always.

— Ashiq
